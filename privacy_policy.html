Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

QalbCare ("the App") is developed by Asif Bhimla. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

Location Data

The App requests access to your device's location to:

  • Calculate accurate prayer times for your current location
  • Determine the Qibla (direction of prayer) from your position

Location data is processed entirely on your device. It is not transmitted to any external server, stored remotely, or shared with any third party.

If you deny location permission, the App will use a default location (Makkah) or a location you set manually in Settings.

Device Sensor Data

The App accesses your device's compass sensor solely to display the Qibla direction. This data is used in real time and is not stored or transmitted.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Account or login credentials
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contacts, photos, or other personal files

3. Data Storage

All user preferences (selected location, calculation method, prayer angle settings, region, and other settings) are stored locally on your device using the operating system's standard preferences storage. No data is sent to or stored on remote servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may connect to the following external service:

  • AlQuran Cloud API (alquran.cloud) — If you enable the optional API mode in Settings, the App fetches Quran verse translations from this service. Only the surah and verse number are sent in the request; no personal data is transmitted. Please refer to the AlQuran Cloud terms for their privacy practices.

The App does not integrate any advertising networks, analytics services, or social media SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The App is suitable for users of all ages and contains no user-generated content, social features, or in-app purchases.

6. Data Security

Since the App stores all data locally on your device and does not transmit personal information, your data is protected by your device's own security measures (passcode, biometrics, encryption).

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Revoke location permission at any time through your device's Settings
  • Delete all App data by uninstalling the App
  • Use the App without granting location access (by setting your location manually)

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
